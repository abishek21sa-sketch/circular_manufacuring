# CIRCULAR-MASS Research Validation Contract

**Null hypothesis H0.** Under common demand/recovery-yield scenarios and the same service constraints, stochastic CIRCULAR-MASS does not improve the virgin-material / shortage / tail-risk trade-off relative to deterministic average-yield planning or a virgin-only plan.

Evidence remains deterministic synthetic formulation validation until recovery-yield distributions, collection volumes, material-passport lineage, and environmental factors are validated on operational data. Optimized circularity is not a realized environmental outcome.

The shipped falsification check deliberately restricts virgin fallback to create positive shortage. It verifies that `eta`, scenario shortage loss, excess variables, and the reported shortage CVaR remain consistent when the tail term is active. The canonical reference's zero-shortage CVaR is therefore not used as evidence of tail-risk improvement.

The evidence artifact also records the deterministic average-yield and virgin-only ablations, a 20-case recycled-content/risk sensitivity grid, solver runtime, objective decomposition, feasibility residual, and the exact claim boundary. A positive or negative objective delta is descriptive for the supplied synthetic scenarios; it is not a causal or field-performance claim.
